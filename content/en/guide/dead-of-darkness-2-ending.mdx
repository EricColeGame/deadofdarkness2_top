## What the Confirmed Story Setup Reveals About the Finale

The premise is unusually specific, and that specificity is a roadmap. Dead of Darkness 2 opens in Pennsylvania, 1985. Jun Takehara lives a quiet life in a trailer deep in the Appalachians, and her only real connection is Felton — an old veteran who was a close friend of her recently deceased father and who has become something of a grandfather figure to her.

During a hunting trip, Jun finds a wounded little girl in a secluded part of the woods. The girl is confused, frightened, and has no memory of who she is or how she got there. With no phone and no transportation, Jun brings her home to tend her wounds. Minutes later, two suspicious policemen arrive, and Jun and Felton are pulled into what the developers describe as a nightmare of unspeakable horrors.
