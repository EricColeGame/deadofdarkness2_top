## Meet Jun Takehara, Felton, and the Girl Who Starts It All

### Jun Takehara — The Player's Anchor

Jun Takehara is the center of the story. She lives alone in a trailer in the Appalachian wilderness, and her only real human connection is Felton. The setup is deliberately isolating: no reliable phone service, no transportation, and no neighbors. When something goes wrong, there is nobody to call.

That isolation is not just atmosphere. It explains why Jun makes the decisions she makes, and it gives the game a believable reason to keep her in danger instead of letting her simply drive to the nearest police station.

### Felton — The Grandfather Figure With a Past

Felton is an old veteran and a close friend of Jun's late father. He promised to look out for her, and he has become something like a grandfather to her. In survival horror terms, he fills the "trusted ally" slot — the person who grounds the protagonist and raises the emotional stakes when things go wrong.

Veteran characters in horror stories usually carry more than they admit. Whether Felton's past connects directly to Goldren Creek's secret is one of the biggest open questions surrounding the Dead of Darkness 2 cast.

### The Wounded Girl — The Mystery at the Center

The inciting incident is a hunting trip. Jun finds a wounded little girl in a secluded part of the woods. The girl is confused and frightened, and she has no idea who she is or how she got there. With no way to call for help, Jun brings her back to the trailer and treats her wounds.

Amnesia is a familiar horror device, but it works here because the girl is the reason the plot moves at all. She is also, notably, unnamed in everything released so far — which suggests her identity is a reveal the game is saving.

### The Two Policemen — The Threat That Arrives at the Door

Minutes after Jun returns home, two suspicious policemen show up. That timing is not accidental. Jun and Felton are pulled into a nightmare of unspeakable horrors, and the policemen are the pivot point where the story shifts from a rescue to a survival situation.

A large cast is not just a storytelling choice in Dead of Darkness 2 — it changes how you play. The developers have confirmed that you can seamlessly switch between up to three characters during certain sections of the game, using their different positions to solve puzzles, conserve resources, and outsmart enemies.

| Positional puzzles | Characters occupy different spots in the world | Reach areas a single protagonist could not |
| Single-player focus | No co-op partner to cover gaps | Every switch is a solo tactical decision |

### Practical Tips for Managing a Large Cast

- **Write it down.** With 28 characters, names blur fast. Keep a short list of who is who and where you last saw them.
- **Read every note.** The store page is explicit that notes, examined objects, and explored rooms hold details about Goldren Creek's secret.
- **Use the shared item box aggressively.** Before a section ends, move your best weapon and healing items to the character most likely to need them.
- **Switch early, not late.** If a puzzle looks like it needs two perspectives, test the switch before you burn resources guessing.

## FAQ About the Dead of Darkness 2 Cast

**Who are the confirmed characters in the Dead of Darkness 2 cast?**

Four are named or clearly described: Jun Takehara, the protagonist; Felton, the old veteran who looks out for her; a wounded little girl Jun finds in the woods; and two suspicious policemen who arrive at her trailer shortly afterward. The developers have confirmed a total roster of 28 characters, most of whom have not been revealed yet.

**How many characters are in Dead of Darkness 2?**

The official count is 28, and each one has a story connected to the larger mystery of Goldren Creek. That does not mean all 28 are playable — only up to three characters can be controlled during certain sections of the game.

**Can you play as more than one character?**

Yes. Dead of Darkness 2 lets you seamlessly switch between up to three characters in specific sections. A shared item box lets you exchange gear between them, which is useful for conserving ammo, moving healing items, and solving puzzles that require two different vantage points.

**Is there a co-op mode or an announced voice cast?**

No co-op has been announced — the game is listed as single-player. Voice actor names have not been revealed either, though full audio is confirmed for English, with subtitle support in French, Italian, German, and Spanish.

**When does Dead of Darkness 2 come out?**

The release date is still listed as "to be announced." Adding it to your wishlist is the simplest way to get notified when Retrofiction Games confirms a launch window.
