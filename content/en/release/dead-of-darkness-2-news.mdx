## Story and Setting: Jun Takehara in Goldren Creek

The premise leans hard into classic survival horror structure: a small, isolated community, a protagonist pulled in against her will, and a mystery that keeps unfolding. The story is set in Pennsylvania in 1985, deep in the Appalachians.

Jun Takehara lives quietly in a trailer in the mountains. Her closest connection is Felton, an old veteran and friend of her late father, who promised to look out for her and has become something like a grandfather figure. During a hunting trip, Jun finds a wounded little girl in a secluded stretch of woods. The girl is frightened and has no memory of who she is or how she got there. With no phone signal and no vehicle, Jun carries her home and treats her injuries.

Minutes later, two suspicious policemen arrive — and Jun and Felton are dragged into something far worse. That setup drives the search for help that leads toward the eerie town of Goldren Creek, where the real horror waits.

## Gameplay Deep Dive: Character Switching, Scarcity, and Weapons

Retrofiction Games is pitching Dead of Darkness 2 as old-school survival horror with modern quality-of-life touches. The systems below are the ones the studio has explicitly confirmed.

**Character switching.** During certain sections, you can swap between up to three characters. Their different positions on the map become puzzle tools: one character can reach a lever while another holds a door, and a shared item box lets you pass gear between them without backtracking the entire map.

**Scarce resources.** Ammo and healing items are limited. You can combine healing items into stronger medkits, attach parts to upgrade weapons, and manage inventory weight so your character stays mobile. That last detail is a meaningful design choice — it forces loadout decisions instead of letting you hoard everything.

**Weapon variety.** The arsenal runs from knives and axes to submachine guns and flamethrowers. Each weapon carries distinct trade-offs across damage, status effects, equip load, and attack speed. A heavy weapon may hit hard but slow you down; a light blade may be fast but nearly useless against certain enemies.

**Exploration and clues.** Files, examinable objects, and environmental details feed the puzzle and progression loop. Clues unlock secrets and reveal hidden pathways, so thorough exploration is effectively a progression mechanic rather than optional flavor.

| Shared item box | Move items between characters instantly | Reduces backtracking frustration |

Mature content is part of the package. The developers describe frequent violence or gore and general mature themes, so this is not a title for younger players — worth knowing before you buy it for someone else.
