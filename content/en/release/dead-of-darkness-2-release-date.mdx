## Story, Setting, and Cast of Dead of Darkness 2

The sequel trades the usual haunted-house setup for something more rural and isolated. It opens in Pennsylvania in 1985, where Jun Takehara lives a quiet life in a trailer deep in the Appalachians. Her closest connection is Felton, an old veteran and friend of her late father who has effectively become a grandfather figure to her.

| Story Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Rural Pennsylvania, 1985 |
| Protagonist | Jun Takehara |
| Key supporting character | Felton, veteran and family friend |
| Inciting incident | Jun finds a wounded, amnesiac girl while hunting |
| Escalation | Two suspicious policemen arrive shortly after |
| Central location | The town of Goldren Creek |
| Cast size | 28 characters with interconnected stories |
| Content rating notes | Frequent violence, gore, mature themes |

During a hunting trip, Jun discovers a wounded little girl in a secluded stretch of woods. The girl cannot remember who she is or how she got there, so Jun brings her home to treat her injuries. Minutes later, two suspicious policemen arrive, and the situation collapses into something far worse. The search for answers pulls Jun and Felton toward Goldren Creek, a town with a secret the game's lore is built around.

That lore is spread across 28 characters, each with their own story tied to the larger mystery. Notes, examinable objects, and room details are all designed to feed into the same puzzle, which means players who read everything will get more out of the experience than those who sprint to the next encounter.
