## Story, Setting, and Cast: Inside Goldren Creek

The sequel takes place in Pennsylvania in 1985. Jun Takehara lives a quiet life in a trailer deep in the Appalachians, and her closest human connection is Felton, an old veteran who was a close friend of her late father and who promised to look out for her. He has become something of a grandfather figure.

During a hunting trip, Jun finds a wounded little girl in a secluded stretch of woods. The girl is confused, frightened, and has no memory of who she is or how she got there. With no phone service and no transportation, Jun carries her home and treats her wounds. Minutes later, two suspicious policemen arrive — and Jun and Felton are pulled into a nightmare that eventually leads toward the eerie town of Goldren Creek.

### How to Prepare Before Launch

- **Finish or replay the first game.** The sequel builds on established lore, and familiar context will make Goldren Creek's reveals land harder.
- **Practice resource discipline now.** If you are used to hoarding in other horror titles, you are already ahead — ammo and healing items are described as scarce.
- **Get comfortable with a controller.** The developers recommend playing with one, and both Xbox and PlayStation controllers are supported.
- **Plan your inventory habits.** A weight system punishes players who carry everything "just in case."
